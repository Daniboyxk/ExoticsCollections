# Exotics Collections — Luxury Design & Brand Guidelines

## Core Brand Identity
- **Name**: Exotics Collections (by Marc Ibáñez, @exoticsbcn)
- **Tagline**: «Exclusividad, diseño y fiabilidad»
- **Stock Axiom**: «No buscamos cualquier coche. Buscamos la unidad perfecta»
- **Confidence Principle**: «Coches que seleccionaríamos para nuestros familiares»
- **Import Slogan**: «Encuentra tu nuevo coche en cualquier parte de Europa»

## Aesthetic Rules
1. **Palette & Visual Excellence**:
   - Backgrounds: `#060709`, `#0a0c10`, `#0f131a`, `#151a24`
   - Accents: Champagne Gold (`#d4a359`, `#eac585`), Emerald (`#10b981`), Amber (`#f59e0b`), Frosted Glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`)
   - Visual inspiration: *Maison Automotive* (`maisonautomotive.es`), *Hollmann International*, *Mechatronik*, *Carugati*
2. **Typography**:
   - Editorial headings: Outfit / Syne / Inter
   - Refined tracking, uppercase small badges, high-contrast readability
3. **Bento Grid Hierarchy**:
   - Hero spotlight cards (2 columns wide) for flagship cars (e.g. Hyundai i30 N Fastback Sky DCT 280 CV)
   - Lateral minimalist status ribbons (4px-6px vertical strip on the left edge with pulse indicator)
   - Dual actions: «Me interesa esta unidad» and «Reservar unidad» (direct WhatsApp)
4. **Editorial Sections**:
   - «¿Por qué hemos seleccionado esta unidad?» with the Curator's Note
   - Video inspection backdrop with technical audit checklist
   - Technical standards: < 7 years, < 90.000 km, official dealer history only, zero accident structural guarantee
5. **Team Roles**:
   - Marc Ibáñez: Founder & Curator
   - Fons: Diagnostic & Inspection Specialist in Germany
   - Beltrán: Acompañamiento y atención al cliente para hacer los procesos más fluidos

## Mandatory Interactive & QA Protocol (In Every Interaction)
1. **Always Audit for Errors**:
   - Check markup, broken image links, missing Sanity CDN assets, CSS layout shifts, and mobile responsiveness.
   - Propose or apply immediate concrete fixes for any defect detected.
2. **Always Ask 1 or 2 Steering Questions**:
   - Conclude each response with 1 or 2 clear, strategic design decisions or preference checks (using `ask_question` or structured choice with a recommended option) to ensure alignment with user expectations and European supercar showroom standards.
